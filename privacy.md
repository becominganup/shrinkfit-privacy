# Privacy Policy for ShrinkFit

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

At ShrinkFit ("we," "our," or "us"), we believe your media and data belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to your privacy, what information is processed, and how it is protected when you use the ShrinkFit iOS application (the "App").

## 1. Local Processing & Data Minimization

ShrinkFit is designed as a "privacy-first" offline utility. 

* **Zero Cloud Storage:** All media processing, including video compression, photo resizing, and audio stripping, occurs 100% locally on your device's hardware. 
* **Zero Telemetry:** We do not track your usage, behavior, or interactions within the app.
* **Zero Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, device IDs, GPS location, IP address, or the contents of your photos and videos to any external servers.

Because your media never leaves your device, we have no access to it, and therefore, we cannot share, sell, or distribute your media to any third party.

## 2. Permissions We Request

To function correctly, the App requests the following on-device permissions:
* **Photo Library Access:** Required solely to select the photos or videos you wish to compress and to save the processed files back to your device. We do not scan or upload your library.

## 3. Third-Party Services (AdMob)

To support the continued development of ShrinkFit, the Free Tier of the App displays banner and interstitial advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. 

If you use the Free Tier, Google AdMob may collect and process certain data to provide and optimize advertisements. This data is collected directly by Google and is governed by Google’s own privacy policies. 

Data collected by Google AdMob may include:
* **Device Information:** Such as OS version, device make and model, and network type.
* **Advertising Identifiers:** Such as the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), subject to your explicit consent via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
* **Usage Data:** Diagnostic data regarding ad performance (e.g., clicks and impressions).

If you purchase any of the **Pro Tier** subscriptions or the Lifetime pass, AdMob is entirely disabled, and absolutely zero third-party SDK network traffic occurs.

For more information on how Google uses this data, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

We comply strictly with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. Upon launching the Free Tier, you will be asked whether you consent to be tracked across other apps and websites for targeted advertising. 
* If you select **"Ask App Not to Track,"** Google AdMob will not access your device's IDFA, and you will receive non-personalized ads. 
* Your choice does not affect the core functionality of the App.

## 5. In-App Purchases

We offer premium features via In-App Purchases (IAP). All payment processing and subscription management is handled securely by **Apple Inc.** via the App Store. We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card information, billing address, or Apple ID. 

Apple provides us with anonymous receipt data solely to verify whether a Pro entitlement should be unlocked on your device.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or Apple's App Store Guidelines. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
